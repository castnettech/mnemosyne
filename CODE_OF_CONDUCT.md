# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful and constructive in feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
